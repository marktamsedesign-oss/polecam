Offline map packs

      
A map pack is a street map of a whole area kept on this phone: roads, buildings, rivers and place names, sharp at every zoom. Get it once on Wi-Fi. Every map pack on this phone is used, all together: each covers its own area, so the map goes from one area to the next as you move, with no switching. When there is no signal the app shows them by itself; the layers button (Offline map) also does. The eye hides a pack on the map (the plan drawings still use it). A road pack (Roads · region) is not a map to look at: the plan drawing (Export → Plan drawing) takes its roads, landmarks, cities / municipalities and barangays from every road pack on this phone that covers the project (a project on the border of two regions gets both), with no internet.
